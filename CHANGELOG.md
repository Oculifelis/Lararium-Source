# Lararium changelog

## 0.3.0 — Call and Answer

- Give a taught Listener one call from a button or a Keeper’s greeting and it plays its eight beats once. Each answer costs one breath. Hold Hollow Thread off hand and sneak-use the Tuning Fork to switch between answering and repeating.
- The fork, Jade and field notes show how to use the new mode. The heart remembers the mode when moved.
- Fixed the marked face emitting redstone on the wrong side. Existing circuits that took output from the marked face can use an unmarked face instead.
- Fixed sneak-use teaching when Minecraft routes the interaction to the fork.

## 0.2.0

- Added Keeper favourites, taught with a Tuning Fork in the main hand and a sample in the off hand
- Added a short redstone greeting when a Keeper collects a favourite
- Changed Keepers to collect favourite items first
- Changed neighbouring Threshold Veils to join into one cloth, up, down and sideways
- Changed the item textures to fuller copper shading and the Draft Brooch to a feather with a copper clasp
- Fixed a heart staying lit after its redstone pulse ended
