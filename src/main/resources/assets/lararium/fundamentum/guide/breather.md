A tall narrow shaft wakes a **Breather**. Try three blocks wide, three deep and seven high. The height must be at least five and at least 1.4 times the wider horizontal span.

Jump inside to catch an upward breath. Sneak to step out of the current. A breath gives a short lift and Slow Falling; continued ascent spends further breaths. Hooking a block pauses the lift so you can settle against a perch.

Ostentum's hooks work on the **Copper Perch**. The **Bobbin Hook** is a short-range, two-hook sidegrade made from Ostentum's Amethyst Hook. Equip it in the Curios hook slot. It reaches 18 blocks and catches the air at maximum range for 2.5 seconds.
