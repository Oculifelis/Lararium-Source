Raise all four forms within 24 blocks of where you stand. Ring the **House Bell**. A Keeper, Breather, Listener and Shelter answer with four notes.

For one minute, consenting nearby inhabitants with the same owner share their stored breaths. A fuller heart passes one breath to a hungrier neighbour. The household conserves its charge; the bell creates none. Each transfer leaves a short visible thread.

You can ring again after a short cooldown. You choose where the homes stand and how they connect to your workshop. Each creature keeps its own form, name, nest and taught phrase.
