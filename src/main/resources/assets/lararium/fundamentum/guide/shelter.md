A broad, low room wakes a **Shelter**. Try six blocks wide, four deep and three high. The longer horizontal span must be at least five, the shorter at least three, with at least 45 empty blocks and a ceiling no higher than three.

Incoming projectiles slow once inside the measured room, spending one breath each. You gain a moment to move aside. Your own and your teammates' shots keep their speed. The projectile remains real; its ammunition and damage are never copied.

The **Draft Brooch** carries a little emergency breath outside. Equip it in a Curios charm slot. A fall over five blocks triggers five seconds of Slow Falling. It holds 16 catches, with a 20-second cooldown that cannot be reset by swapping brooches. Craft with an amethyst shard to recharge it.
