A compact enclosed space wakes a **Keeper**. Try a room three blocks wide, three high and three deep. Put the heart on its floor.

Toss an item inside your own Keeper's room. It gathers the item into its nine-stack nest, spending one breath for a successful collection. Use the heart with an empty hand to retrieve a stack. Other players' discarded items are left alone.

The nest stays safe while the room sleeps. Breaking the heart returns its contents. Hoppers and other machines can work with weaving inputs and output; they cannot drain the nest.

## Familiar things

- Hold the **Tuning Fork in your main hand** and a sample item in your off hand.
- Sneak-use your awake Keeper. It remembers the item type and leaves the sample with you.
- It collects favourite offerings first. Names, enchantments and other item details do not change what it favours.
- Other offerings are still welcome. If a favourite cannot fit, the Keeper tries the other offerings.
- Sneak-use with an empty off hand to forget the preference.

When it gathers a favourite, the heart brightens and its ribbons open for **one second**. The unmarked faces give a strength-15 redstone signal during that greeting. Connect a lamp, a door circuit or a Listener's teaching input to hear the arrival elsewhere. Further favourite collections extend the greeting; they do not queue extra signals. A full nest or an empty heart gives no false greeting.

The favourite survives saving, remodelling and moving the heart. Your fork's measurement and Jade show it to you. Each successful collection still costs one breath; the nest remains private.
