An enclosed passage that goes all the way around a solid pillar wakes a **Listener**. The passage needs at least two blocks of headroom. Try a five-by-five interior with a central pillar reaching its three-block-high ceiling.

The heart's marked front face is its input. Sneak-use the Tuning Fork to record **eight half-second beats**. Change the input with a lever or another redstone source while it listens. After four seconds it repeats the taught high and low beats through the other faces.

Repeating costs one breath per phrase. Sneak-use the fork to pause or resume. Hold redstone dust in your off hand while sneak-using the fork to teach a fresh phrase. The previous complete phrase remains safe until teaching finishes. The phrase survives saving and remodelling. Fieldframe can read the ordinary redstone output.

## Call and Answer

Teach your Listener a rhythm. Then hold **Hollow Thread** in your off hand and sneak-use the **Tuning Fork** on its heart. The thread stays in your hand. The Listener now waits for a call.

Run a repeater into the marked face. A button on that line makes the Listener play its eight beats once for **one breath**. Connect a lamp to an unmarked face to see the answer. For a delivery bell, wire your Keeper's greeting into the same line.

A held signal counts as one call. Calls during an answer or with an empty heart pass unheard. Let the input go low before calling again. Sneak-use the fork with an empty off hand to pause or resume. Use Hollow Thread again to return to repeating.
